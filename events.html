Regional CA Spring Retreat

* * *

February 28th-March 2nd

Molalla Retreat Center, 36208 S Molalla Forest Rd, Molalla, OR 97038

* * *

Come join the whole Northwest C.A. family for our awesome winter retreat in the beautiful Oregon wilderness. Our theme this year is "BE STILL AND KNOW: PEACE WITH GOD, WITH OURSELVES, WITH ONE ANOTHER, AND WITH THE WORLD." In addition to great teaching, worship, food, and fellowship, the recreational options include basketball, disc golf, ropes course, gaga ball, game room, ultimate frisbee, and swimming.

Cost: $100 - BUT please don't let this stop you from coming! We have scholarships available! Please contact Richard Feredinos at richard.feredinos@gmail.com.

If you have any questions, please feel free to email our club president, Serenna Walter (slthorsen@willamette.edu).
